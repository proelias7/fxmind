  1. Scroll to see how it works↓
  2. 01

    Learns from every agent.

    Claude Code, Cursor, Codex, Gemini CLI, Copilot and OpenCode share one memory. What one agent decides, fixes and verifies becomes a signal.

  3. 02

    Jev AI decides what matters.

    Optional. Jev classifies the task, picks which memories matter and which tool to use, and what is worth saving.

  4. 03

    Creates memories with evidence.

    New facts become neurons wired to the code: file, line and SHA-256 as proof.

  5. 04

    Queries the graph.

    Jev points to the right memory, and the question travels the connections and comes back with just the right snippet: 242 tokens instead of 1.3 million, in 73 ms.

  6. 05

    Fewer tokens, faster answers.

    5,400× fewer tokens than searching and reading the code, and 10.8× faster searches with a warm cache.
